About

A chemical engineering capstone, turned into a startup.

The story so far

RenewBlocks started inside a Chemical Engineering program at Baku Higher Oil School, where three waste streams kept turning up in local industrial data — shredded plastic, incinerator ash, and limestone quarry dust — none of them with a clear second use nearby.

The first lab formula came out of coursework and independent testing. It placed second at BHOS's 7th International Student Research Conference in April 2026, moved into the AIM YETI startup program that summer, and is now running as a SOCAR capstone project.

The idea is deliberately narrow: not a load-bearing block, not a replacement for every kind of masonry — a specific product for interior partition walls, built from material that's already local. We're at an early stage. The lab formula is validated; a full-scale block hasn't been produced yet.

Roadmap

Where we've been, where we're headed.

Done

Lab formula validated

Solid lab specimens tested for compressive strength. 2nd place at BHOS's student research conference; AIM YETI and SOCAR Capstone participation.

Next

Cure optimization & standards filing

Lab batch testing to lock the cure parameters, alongside filing for the AZS 418-2010 Azerbaijan wall-stone standard.

Then

Sumgait pilot

First full-scale hollow block production runs, with independent lab testing on the actual commercial block.

Later

First deliveries, then certification

Private residential projects don't require AZS certification, so early deliveries can start there. Certification and larger contracts follow.
